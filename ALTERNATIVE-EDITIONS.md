## Kautsky, Erfurt commentary (1892)

- Bohn, 1910: the translator's note at https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Class_Struggle explicitly says the text was compressed to two-thirds of the eighth German edition (1907). MIA and Wikirouge reproduce this edition; a different host alone is not an alternative translation.
- De Leon, chapter III, *The Capitalist Class*: https://www.marxists.org/archive/kautsky/1911/cap-class/index.htm. Source and translation credits appear in the index. The English pamphlet is adapted, so the catalog does not claim parallel text. Related chapter IV and V pamphlets: https://libcom.org/article/class-struggle-erfurt-program-daniel-de-leon-translation . Scans are leads only; no target PDF ingestion is implemented here.
- Neumann / SPGB, chapter III: https://www.worldsocialism.org/spgb/kautsky-on-capitalist-class-1908/ . The page describes the translation as approved by the author; it also reproduces passages serialized in *Socialist Standard* (e.g. https://www.worldsocialism.org/spgb/socialist-standard/1900s/1909/no-55-may-1909/the-capitalist-class-by-karl-kautsky-continued-5/). Chapter II: https://www.worldsocialism.org/spgb/kautsky-on-working-class-1908/ . Bibliographic attribution: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Author:Karl_Kautsky . Outside the current resolver's supported document providers; not a receipt target yet. No allowlist expansion in this change.
- Florence Baldwin: pamphlets listed in https://content-lists.sl.nsw.gov.au/tabular-list/peace-and-labour-pamphlets-1910-1919-box-1-2 . Catalog holdings are not an accessible text.

## Victor Serge

*Thirty Years After the Russian Revolution*, Michel Bolsey translation, included in *Russia Twenty Years After* (Humanities Press, 1996). https://solidarity-us.org/p839/ confirms the edition. No verified accessible complete text in this research; bibliographic lead only.

## Trotsky, Terrorism and Communism

https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/38982/pg38982-images.html is a digital transcription of the 1922 Workers Party of America printing. https://wikirouge.net/texts/en/Terrorism_and_Communism identifies the US printing behind its transcription. Compare the underlying edition and literal text before treating another host as new evidence. Do not assume a different translation from a different title or year alone.
